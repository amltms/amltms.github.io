Privacy Policy

Bot Message · Last updated 11 November 2026

Bot Message is a small automated assistant that replies to Instagram direct messages sent to @amltms. It is run personally by Amal Thomas, not by a company. This page explains what happens to your messages.

What it receives

When you message @amltms, Instagram (Meta) passes the bot:

  • the text of your message and when you sent it;
  • your Instagram-scoped ID: a number Instagram assigns to you for this account, not your username;
  • the type of anything else you send, such as a photo, voice note or shared post. The bot is told only what kind of thing it was. It does not download, view or listen to it.

How it is used

Your messages are used only to write a reply to you. They are processed by an AI model that runs on Amal’s own computer. They are not sent to ChatGPT, Claude or any other third-party AI service. Replies go back to you through Instagram’s official API.

Replies may be written automatically. Amal may also read your messages and reply personally.

Storage

Your recent conversation (your messages and the bot’s replies) is stored on that same computer so that replies make sense in context. It is kept until you ask for it to be deleted or the bot is switched off for good.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared with anyone or used for advertising. Instagram handles your messages under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Send @amltms a message on Instagram asking for your data to be deleted. Your stored conversation will be removed within 30 days, and you’ll get a reply to confirm it. You can also simply stop messaging the account.

Contact

For any questions about this policy, message @amltms on Instagram.
